The 12-Point Growth Audit Playbook

This is the exact framework our strategists run on every prospective client before we ever pitch a plan. Twelve checks, three categories, about ten minutes — grab a pen and be honest with your answers.

Positioning

  1. 1
    Write your positioning in one sentence.No jargon, no "leading provider of." If a customer can't repeat it back to you, it isn't working yet.
  2. 2
    Name your three real competitors.Not aspirational ones — who your buyer actually compares you to, and the one reason they'd pick you.
  3. 3
    Compare your homepage headline to your positioning sentence.If they don't say the same thing, your best ad spend is undercut by your own site.
  4. 4
    Ask three recent customers why they bought.If none of them repeat your marketing language, you've found your positioning gap.

Funnel

  1. 5
    Map every step from first touch to signed contract.Count the steps. More than five is a red flag worth investigating.
  2. 6
    Time how long it takes a new visitor to find your next step.Pricing, a demo link, a contact form — more than 30 seconds is too long.
  3. 7
    Audit your form fields.Every field beyond name and email typically costs you 5–10% of conversions.
  4. 8
    Find where leads go quiet.Pull your last 20 opportunities and mark the exact stage each one stalled at.

Channel Mix

  1. 9
    List every active channel and the pipeline it produced last quarter.Pipeline, not traffic. Traffic without pipeline is a vanity metric.
  2. 10
    Cut anything with zero qualified pipeline in 90 days.Traffic alone doesn't earn a channel its budget.
  3. 11
    Check if your top channel is a rented one.An ad platform or algorithm-fed channel with no owned-audience backup is a single point of failure.
  4. 12
    Confirm you can attribute a closed deal to its first touch.If you can't trace it, your reporting is a guess dressed up as a dashboard.

Found more gaps than you expected? That's normal — and fixable.
